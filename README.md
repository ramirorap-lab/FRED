# Fred — Your Film Friend

Fred is a film recommendation app that actually knows what it's talking about.

Tell Fred what you're in the mood for tonight. Fred finds you 2 films and 1 series worth watching — curated by AI, informed by IMDB, Rotten Tomatoes, and the taste of the greatest directors alive.

**[fred-psi.vercel.app](https://fred-psi.vercel.app)**

---

### What Fred does

- Picks 2 films + 1 series based on your mood and streaming platforms
- One pick is always a **Director's Pick** — a film chosen as a personal favorite by Scorsese, Villeneuve, Gerwig, Tarantino, and 15 other filmmakers
- **Ask Fred** anything — best film about obsession, something Italian, under 90 minutes, no superheroes
- Mark films as **Seen it** and Fred never repeats them
- Save picks to your **Watchlist**
- Sign in with a magic link — no password, Fred remembers everything

### Platforms supported

Netflix · Prime Video · Hulu · Max · Apple TV+ · Disney+ · Peacock
